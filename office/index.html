Sim Office

Live and work at Seaside Labs, a software company in a small American city. Pick who you are: each has a job, a home and people of their own. Commute, grab a coffee, shop and pay the bills. The first 15 days have missions (finish them all for a bonus), then it's free play. Be late or miss work too often and you'll be let go.
